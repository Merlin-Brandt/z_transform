While **z** rotates along the unit circle over time, the complex summands of the Z-Transform for the first three harmonics are continually visualized as blue dots on black background, resulting in interesting flower-patterns revealing the intrinsics of

$$\mathcal{Z}\{x[n]\}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} x[n]\cdot z^{-n}$$
